Ángel Merino Hernández

PhD Candidate and R&D Engineer working on online advertising, privacy, social media data, and Green IT

I combine empirical research and engineering to study how social platforms and advertising systems use public and behavioural data. My work spans large-scale data collection, reverse engineering of advertising APIs, semantic analysis with embeddings and vector databases, privacy metrics, and sustainability frameworks for digital systems.
