Ángel Merino Hernández

PhD Candidate researching online advertising, privacy, and social media data

I study how social platforms and advertising systems use public and behavioural data. My work combines large-scale data collection, computational social science, and empirical measurement to analyse privacy risks, user uniqueness, and targeting practices in real online systems.
