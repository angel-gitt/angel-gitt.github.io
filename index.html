PhD Candidate · Universidad Carlos III de Madrid

Making digital systems a little more observable.

I am a PhD Candidate and researcher in the Department of Telematic Engineering at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. My work focuses on online advertising, user privacy in social networks, and automated data collection and analysis for studying how platform systems actually behave in practice.

Research focus

Questions worth measuring carefully

My work sits at the intersection of platform studies, empirical measurement, and computational analysis of online systems.

Online advertising & privacy

Studying advertising infrastructures, user targeting, and privacy risks, including how apparently generic public attributes can still enable precise targeting.

advertising systems nanotargeting privacy

Automated data collection

Designing reproducible pipelines for collecting and analysing data from online systems, with attention to scale, validity, and transparency.

measurement automation reproducibility

Social networks & user data

Investigating how social platforms structure visibility, uniqueness, and user information, especially when public data can be repurposed in unexpected ways.

social media user uniqueness non-PII
