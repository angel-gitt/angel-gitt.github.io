Ángel Merino Hernández

Researcher in Social Media Data, Privacy, and Online Advertising

I study the intersection of online behavioural data, platform ecosystems, and digital advertising. My work combines computational social science and data analytics to understand how data practices shape individual experiences, privacy risks, and broader societal outcomes.

Publications Talks & Tutorials Research Portal Contact
Current focus Measuring how social platforms and advertising systems use public data, structure visibility, and create privacy and targeting risks in practice.
